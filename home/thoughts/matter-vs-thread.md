If you’ve spent any time researching smart home devices lately, you’ve probably noticed two buzzwords that keep appearing side by side: Thread and Matter. They sound related — and they are — but they aren’t the same thing. In fact, many people mistake one for the other, or assume they must choose between them when building a smart home.


The truth is simpler and far more helpful: Thread and Matter were designed to work together, each solving a different part of the smart home puzzle. And when you bring both into Homey Pro, you get a smooth, modern, and future-ready home that feels wonderfully effortless.

Let’s break it all down in a clear, friendly way, so the next time you see “Thread” or “Matter” printed on a box, you’ll know exactly what it means for your home.

## What Thread Actually Is
Thread is a wireless communication protocol built specifically for smart home devices. Instead of every device crowding your Wi-Fi router, Thread devices connect to one another to form a secure, self-healing "mesh" network. This ensures your connection is reliable, your battery-operated devices last longer, and your Wi-Fi stays fast for everything else.

If your home is full of door sensors, buttons, switches, and lights, Thread gives these devices their own dedicated highway to communicate on. And because Homey Pro includes a built-in Thread radio, you don’t need extra smart hubs — Thread devices connect directly and immediately strengthen your network.


## What Matter Actually Is
Matter is the universal language that allows your devices to understand each other. It solves the compatibility headache by ensuring products from different brands communicate seamlessly. With Matter, a single device can work across multiple platforms — like Homey, Apple Home, and Google Home— simultaneously, without needing to be re-paired.

It also prioritizes local control, meaning your lights and plugs respond instantly even if your internet goes down. When you see the Matter logo on products like the Nanoleaf Essentials Bulb or Eve Energy plug, you know they will be easy to connect and reliable to use with any Matter controller.

## Thread Is the Road. Matter Is the Language.
The simplest way to understand the relationship is with a metaphor.

Imagine your smart home is a city. Thread builds the roads — providing the strong, reliable paths for data to travel. Matter sets the traffic rules — providing the common language and signage so every vehicle knows exactly what to do. While they perform different jobs, they work together: Thread handles the connection, and Matter handles the conversation.
